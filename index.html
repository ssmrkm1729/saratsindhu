Sarat Sindhu Mukhopadhyay

Indian Statistical Institute, Bangalore · CV

Hello, welcome to my homepage. I am currently a PhD student in Quality, Reliability and Operations Research at Indian Statistical Institute, Bangalore. My research interests broadly focus on statistical modeling and inference for life-testing and reliability models, particularly Step-Stress Accelerated Life Testing (SSALT) under non-constant hazard rate settings. I have worked on reliability modeling under different experimental and censoring schemes, including progressive censoring, repeated Type-II censoring, and competing risks, with an emphasis on dependent failure mechanisms. Currently, I am working on degradation modeling under stressed conditions for reliability assessment.
